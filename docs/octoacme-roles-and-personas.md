# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Work closely with **QA/Testing Lead** on test strategy and acceptance criteria validation
- Partner with **Technical Lead/Architect** on design decisions and technical risk mitigation
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on feature specifications and priorities

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Report to **Product Lead** on strategic alignment and major decisions
- Work with **Project Managers** on scheduling and scope trade-offs
- Collaborate with **Developers** and **QA/Testing Lead** on acceptance criteria refinement

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Escalate business-impacting risks to **Product Lead** and **Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder**
- Coordinate with **Scrum Master/Agile Coach** on team capacity and process adherence
- Partner with all team members on status tracking and risk management

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy, test planning, and acceptance validation for projects. They collaborate with product and development teams to ensure features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans and QA strategies
- Design and execute unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
- Validate acceptance criteria and feature acceptance
- Identify and document defects with clear reproduction steps
- Contribute to Definition of Done (DoD) criteria
- Perform security and performance testing as needed
- Work with developers on test automation and CI integration

### Goals
- Ensure high quality and reliability before release
- Reduce post-production defects and support burden
- Accelerate feedback loops through automated testing
- Build customer confidence in product quality

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and acceptance criteria reviews
- Daily standup status on blockers and test coverage
- Bug reports and test result summaries
- QA strategy discussions and retrospectives

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Developers** on test automation and coverage strategies
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** to understand acceptance criteria and user scenarios
- Work with **Technical Lead/Architect** on test infrastructure and performance testing needs
- Report quality metrics to **Project Manager** for release readiness assessment

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical design decisions, manage technical risks, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with architecture standards. They mentor developers and advocate for technical excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers on coding standards and best practices
- Review and approve significant technical decisions
- Ensure integration with existing systems and platforms
- Contribute to technology stack and infrastructure decisions
- Participate in capacity planning and performance optimization

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and maintenance burden
- Build team capability through mentoring and design guidance
- Reduce integration risk and post-launch issues

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Code review feedback and mentoring
- Technical risk identification in planning and standups
- Design document collaboration

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Mentor **Developers** on architectural patterns and technical standards
- Advise **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- Collaborate with **QA/Testing Lead** on testability and performance testing
- Escalate technical risks to **Project Manager** and **Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder** as needed

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Leads provide strategic product direction, approve major decisions, and champion the product vision. They work across Product Managers, stakeholders, and leadership to align projects with business strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Set strategic product direction and vision
- Approve major feature and roadmap decisions
- Align projects with business goals and customer needs
- Resolve prioritization conflicts between projects
- Champion product outcomes to leadership and stakeholders
- Guide product strategy evolution based on data and market feedback
- Escalate business-impacting decisions and risks

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver strategic business value
- Maximize alignment between product vision and execution
- Build customer and stakeholder confidence in product direction
- Enable data-informed strategic decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Strategic planning sessions and roadmap reviews
- Monthly stakeholder and leadership briefings
- Escalation and decision gates
- Retrospectives to capture strategic learnings

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Guide **Product Managers** on strategic priorities and trade-off decisions
- Provide strategic context to **Project Managers** for planning and milestone definition
- Escalate strategic risks to **Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder**
- Review outcomes with all team members during retrospectives and planning gates

---

## Sponsor/Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors are business leaders who provide funding, priority setting, and escalation authority for projects. They represent key stakeholder interests and ensure alignment with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide executive sponsorship and air cover for projects
- Approve funding and resource allocation
- Set business priorities and make go/no-go decisions
- Escalate business-impacting issues and risks
- Communicate project importance to broader organization
- Ensure alignment with business strategy and compliance requirements
- Participate in project approval gates and major reviews

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Maintain organizational alignment and strategy execution
- Remove executive-level blockers and barriers
- Build stakeholder confidence and transparency

### Typical Communication
- Project approval and funding decisions
- Executive escalations and risk briefings
- Monthly or quarterly business reviews
- Retrospective reviews of project outcomes and impact

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Receive escalations and risk briefings from **Project Manager**
- Review strategic progress and decisions with **Product Lead**
- Provide final approval authority for major project gates and direction changes
- Communicate project success and learnings to the broader organization

---

## Scrum Master/Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on process adherence and continuous improvement. They enable team autonomy and accountability within the agreed framework.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments preventing progress
- Coach team on agile practices and Definition of Done
- Maintain sprint backlog and track velocity metrics
- Identify and escalate process blockers
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement culture
- Adapt ceremonies and processes based on team feedback

### Goals
- Enable consistent sprint execution and predictability
- Improve team velocity and effectiveness over time
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Create a culture of continuous learning and improvement

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members on blockers
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Process improvement suggestions and metrics reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Project Manager** on team capacity planning and timeline adjustments
- Coach all team members (**Developers**, **Product Managers**, **QA/Testing Lead**, etc.) on agile practices
- Help remove impediments by coordinating with **Technical Lead/Architect**, **Product Lead**, and other stakeholders
- Facilitate communication and transparency across all team members
